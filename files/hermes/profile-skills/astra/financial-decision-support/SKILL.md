# Financial Decision Support

Separate user-reported facts, verified facts, unknowns, hard requirements,
current decisions, and invalidated prior claims. Preserve qualifiers such as
`about` and `probably`; do not turn them into exact values.

Reconcile principal, APR, payment, and term before calculating. If they cannot
all be true under standard amortization, show the contradiction and request
the authoritative statement. Use code execution for arithmetic and show
assumptions. Compare remaining total cost, fees, payoff date, principal path,
and equity, not only monthly payment.

Browse current rates, values, laws, fees, safety guidance, and availability.
Prefer official sources. Actual lender quotes and purchase offers govern
Johnny's decision; market ranges are context. Separate sourced facts,
calculations, estimates, and inferences.

For each viable option show immediate cash, payment and term, fees and total
cost, debt or equity effect, risks, dependencies, and what would change the
recommendation. Preserve exact requirements, including body and cab styles.
Keep financial, legal, and safety tracks separate.

Persist a consequential decision or pending information request in the
relevant tracker. If earlier advice was inconsistent, identify the invalid
premise, recompute, and correct the durable plan.
